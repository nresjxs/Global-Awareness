Why Does This Matter?

Social injustice occurs when individuals or communities experience unfair treatment, unequal opportunities, discrimination, or barriers to basic rights and resources.

These issues are complex and often connected. War can contribute to displacement, displacement can create barriers to education, and economic inequality can increase vulnerability to other forms of injustice.

War & Conflict

Understanding how armed conflict affects people, communities, and societies around the world.

What Is War & Conflict?

War and armed conflict can occur for many different political, territorial, economic, historical, and social reasons.

The consequences of conflict can extend far beyond the battlefield, affecting civilians, families, education, healthcare, infrastructure, and access to basic resources.

Who Is Affected?

Civilians can experience displacement, disruption to education, separation from family members, and difficulty accessing essential services.

Why Does It Matter Globally?

Conflict in one region can have consequences beyond national borders through displacement, humanitarian needs, economic effects, and international responses.
